2. Categories of data collected

The App does not show ads. No name, email, address, phone number, contacts, precise GPS location, health or financial data is collected.

User content (reviews, ratings, rankings) is stored on device and/or synced via iCloud / CloudKit (Apple). The controller does not run a proprietary reviews backend and does not access your personal library contents.

3. Consent (GDPR / ePrivacy)

In the EEA, UK or Switzerland the App may show a Google UMP consent form (e.g. for analytics). You can change choices in Settings → Privacy → Manage consent when available. The App does not request App Tracking Transparency for advertising tracking.

4. Retention

  • Reviews/ratings: on device / iCloud until you delete them or uninstall.
  • Analytics/crashes: per Google retention (typically ~2 months / ~90 days).
  • UMP preferences: stored on device.

5. Recipients

  • Google (Firebase, UMP)
  • Apple (iCloud / CloudKit, iTunes Search)
  • TMDB, Open Library, Google Books

Data is not sold. Firebase reports to the controller are aggregated.

6. Transfers outside the EU

Providers may process data in the US and elsewhere under SCCs and, where applicable, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may access, rectify, erase, restrict, object, port data, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint (Italy: garanteprivacy.it). Contact ranknrate.app@gmail.com.
